# Code of conduct

MedHarbor is about health, and many people in our community have lived through illness, displacement or moving between countries. We expect everyone who takes part in our repositories, issues and discussions to keep that in mind.

## Be respectful

- Be kind and patient. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Respect different languages, backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Keep personal and medical data out

- Never post real personal data or real medical documents, yours or anyone else's: no names, dates of birth, diagnoses, prescriptions, test results or scans.
- Use made-up examples only. Anything that looks like real health data will be removed.

## This is not a medical service

MedHarbor does not give medical advice, and nothing in these repositories is medical advice.

**In an emergency, call your local emergency number. In the European Union, dial 112.**

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks this code of conduct, or you feel unsafe, email **[info@medharborai.com](mailto:info@medharborai.com)**. We will read every report, treat it confidentially and act on it. Possible actions range from a warning to removing content or blocking a person from our repositories.
