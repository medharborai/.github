<h3 align="center">Your whole medical history. In your doctor's language.</h3>

MedHarbor is an AI medical passport in development. People who move between countries often carry discharge letters, lab results and prescriptions in several languages that their new doctor cannot read. MedHarbor will turn those documents into one clear patient card that any doctor can read in their own language, with every fact linked back to the original page.

> **Stage:** in development. The concept and the patient card design are ready. The product does not exist yet and does not process any health data today.

### What the AI does

- Reads photos, scans and PDFs of medical documents in any language, including handwritten notes
- Recognises the document type: discharge letter, lab report, prescription, vaccination record
- Picks out diagnoses, medication, allergies, test results and procedures, with international codes (ICD-10, ATC, LOINC)
- Shows how sure it is about each fact and links every fact to its page in the original
- Creates the patient card in the doctor's language, to share as a PDF, a QR code or a link that expires

### What the AI does not do

- It does not diagnose, interpret results or recommend treatment
- It does not make medical decisions: patients and doctors do
- It is not trained on user data, and health data is never sold or shared
- It does not show ads or promote medicines

Allergies and medication always need the patient's confirmation before a card is shared.
